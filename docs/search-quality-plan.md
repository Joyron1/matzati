### A2 Relevance, category and featured guard (lib/ranking/rank.ts, config.ts)

Add `relevance(p, filters)` in [0, 1], made of:

- **termPos:** the token index of the earliest product-term match. It scores 1.0 at index 2 or less and falls linearly to 0 at TYPE_GATE.windowTokens.
- **headNoun:** 0 when another product noun follows the match within headGap, or an unsearched object modifier (car, seat, bike, motorcycle) comes before it.
- **coverage:** the share of stemmed keywords_en tokens found in the title.

Category outlier rule:

- Find the mode of category.secondName among passers, needing at least 3 members and at least 50% share.
- A passer whose category.firstId differs from the mode's is demoted after all non-outliers.
- Never drop it: category names are noisy (a USB cable filed under Security & Protection).

Sort order: non-outlier tier, then score plus WEIGHTS.relevance times relevance (start at 2.0 and tune in replay), then commission as a tie, then id.

Featured guard (pipeline.ts, after explainRange):

- Trigger when why_he starts with "אביזר משלים" or contains `לא ${product_he}`.
